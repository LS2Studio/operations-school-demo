The problem

Most business programs teach you to start. Nobody teaches you to operate.

You can get a business plan, a logo, and a website in a weekend. But what happens when you need to hire someone? When your books are a mess? When you're drowning in admin work instead of doing what you love?

What other programs teach

  • How to write a business plan
  • How to get an LLC
  • How to make a logo
  • How to post on social media
  • How to pitch investors

What Operations School teaches

  • How to build systems that run without you
  • How to create SOPs your team actually follows
  • How to use technology to save time
  • How to manage finances and cash flow
  • How to hire, onboard, and manage people
The program

Get Your Business Legit in 9 weeks.

Our flagship program takes you from scattered to systematic. You'll leave with real systems, real SOPs, and real clarity on how to run your business like a business.

Weeks 1-3
📋

Foundation

Business structure, legal basics, financial setup, and the mindset shift from hustler to CEO. Get your house in order.

Weeks 4-6
⚙️

Systems

Build your standard operating procedures, choose your tech stack, automate the repetitive work, and create workflows that actually work.

Weeks 7-9
🚀

Scale

Hiring and team building, customer experience, marketing systems, and a 90-day action plan to keep the momentum going after graduation.
